PLC Programming & SCADA

Expert programming for all major PLC platforms. From simple sequence logic to complex multi-axis motion control, we handle the full software lifecycle — design, coding, simulation, commissioning.

PLC Panel Repair & AMC

Fast turnaround repair services for faulty PLC panels, drives and automation equipment. Annual Maintenance Contracts available for guaranteed uptime across your plant.

  • Panel rewiring & upgrades
  • Preventive maintenance visits
  • 24×7 emergency breakdown support

PCB Test Setup Design

Custom test fixture and station design for electronics manufacturers. We work from your Gerber files, BOM and test specifications to deliver production-ready ICT and FCT setups.

  • Fixture design from Gerbers

  • Pneumatic actuation systems
  • Test software development
  • Yield analysis & optimization

Camera-Based Automation

Machine vision and AI-powered inspection systems for quality control on production lines. Detect defects, verify labels, read codes and measure dimensions — automatically, at production speed.

  • Presence / absence verification

  • OCR — label & date code reading
  • Dimensional measurement
